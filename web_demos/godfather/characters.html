Character Comparison

Vito Corleone

  • • Built empire through respect and loyalty
  • • Values family above all else
  • • Wise and patient leader
  • • Believes in honor and tradition
  • • Seeks balance between business and family

Michael Corleone

  • • Inherits empire through necessity
  • • Becomes increasingly isolated
  • • Ruthless and calculating
  • • Driven by protection and control
  • • Sacrifices family for power

"The difference between Vito and Michael is that Vito never forgot where he came from, while Michael never knew where he was going."
